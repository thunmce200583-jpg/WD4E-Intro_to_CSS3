Understanding Coffee Beans

Primary Bean Varieties

Arabica Varieties

Arabica beans grow at high altitudes, offering complex sweetness, vibrant acidity, and fruity notes.

Robusta Origins

Robusta delivers a strong, earthy taste profile with higher caffeine content and rich crema production.

Roast Profiles

Light Roast

Retains original origin characteristics, bright acidity, and light body style.

Medium Roast

Offers a balanced body with subtle caramel undertones and smooth finish.

Dark Roast

Bold, smoky flavors with heavy body and low overall acidity levels.
